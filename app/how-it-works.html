Changes needed?

An approver sends back a clear message and flags the sections to fix. The vendor resubmits and all approvals restart. Bank changes always need a new call-back.

Not approved?

Rejection closes the request. The requester sees the internal reason; the vendor receives a neutral update.

SAP unavailable or an error?

Temporary failures are retried. A master data administrator handles remaining errors and retries safely without creating a second vendor.

Clear responsibilities

One person can act on only one approval step per submission. Auditors have read-only access. Working-day targets exclude weekends and are reminders, not guarantees.
